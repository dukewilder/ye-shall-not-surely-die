# Ye Shall Not Surely Die

### A Defence of the Accused

**L. Calaris**

---

For twenty-five centuries the western world has kept a file on one defendant, and he has never been allowed to answer it. He kept no records of his own, so every word ever set down about him was set down by the parties prosecuting him. They set it down carefully, and they kept it.

This book reads that file.

The Hebrew word underneath the whole apparatus is a common noun. It occurs twenty-seven times and carries the definite article in seventeen of them, the way *the priest* does, or *the king*. On seven occasions it describes a human being, and twice it names a man the text says God raised up for the purpose. It is the title of a post, and the earliest material gives its holder no origin, no genealogy, no rebellion, and no name.

Everything after that was assembled out of documents about other people. A taunt over a dead Babylonian king, heading still attached, supplied the fall from heaven and, through Jerome's Latin, the name Lucifer. A Greek word ordinary enough to be used of women who talked too much supplied what most of Europe would come to call him. A serpent in a garden supplied the temptation, fastened to him centuries later by two verses in other books. Each transfer can be dated from the archives of the men who made it.

Six properties were charged against him, and each turns out to be one his accusers held in the highest degree and could least afford to examine. Pride was alleged by the most rigidly ranked institution in Europe. Appetite was catalogued in technical detail by men under vows against it. The demand for worship was entered by the only party in the transaction receiving any.

A portrait of that kind has to be got into a court before it can kill anybody, and getting it there took the collapse of the ordeal, a procedure that needed no accuser, a rule of proof requiring two eyewitnesses to an act nobody could witness, and the one proof remaining that could always be produced. Between forty-five and sixty thousand people were executed on the strength of it, most of them poor, most of them women.

Over nine hundred years his accusers then took the file apart. A Benedictine archbishop revoked his rights over the captive dead. Textual scholars detached the serpent. Exegetes returned the morning star to the king of Babylon. Linguists returned the noun to its grammar. None acted in his interest, none worked on the same problem, and by the end every property he had been assigned had been withdrawn by the institution that assigned it.
